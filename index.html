What makes it different

  • Budget in, weekend out: one total for two, every card shows what is left.
  • Swipe to decide: fast, low-effort choice for people who are "just looking".
  • Taste learning you can see: each swipe updates visible preferences; one tap to reset.
  • Decide together: couples and friends get only mutual matches, which removes the "send me the link" back-and-forth.
  • Step up, not just cheaper: each card offers the next better hotel in the same city, matching how people already book.
  • Top 3: turns browsers into a weekly owned channel instead of paid retargeting.
